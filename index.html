Questions before applying

Change should feel clear, not risky.

I do not have time.

That is exactly why Seinote exists: to reduce time spent on administrative work and give you more time for clients.

Does AI replace judgment?

No. Seinote uses AI as a support tool. The professional remains responsible for reviewing and finalizing clinical information.

What if my current software works?

You can experience Seinote during the free Early Access period and decide whether the workflow is right for your practice.

What about privacy?

Privacy and responsible data handling are core product priorities. No patient information belongs in this application form.

FAQ

What professionals usually ask first.

What is Seinote?

Seinote is an operational platform for mental health professionals, focused on clinical notes, scheduling, video sessions and billing workflows.

Who is Early Access for?

Psychologists, psychotherapists, counsellors, therapists, social workers and other mental health professionals in Canada, especially independent or early-stage practices.

Is Seinote already available to use?

Yes. Early Access is the real-use phase for selected professionals, not a fictional waitlist.

When do I become a Seinote Early Access Partner?

During the free period you are an Early Access Applicant. After subscribing, you become a Seinote Early Access Partner.

Do I need to attend meetings or give a testimonial?

No. Feedback is welcome, but there are no fixed meetings, testimonial requirements or image-use obligations.

Is there a mobile app?

No. Seinote is currently available as a web platform.
