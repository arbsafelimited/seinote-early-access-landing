Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Seinote Early Access marketing website handles information, analytics and advertising-related data.

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Who Operates This Website

This website is operated for Seinote. The public contact identified for Seinote in this project is hello@seinote.ca.

This repository does not publish a separate corporate legal name or mailing address for Seinote. This policy therefore does not invent or display one.

Scope

This policy applies to the public marketing website at marketing.seinote.ca. It does not describe the full logged-in Seinote application at seinote.com, except where this website redirects visitors there to apply or sign up.

Information This Website May Collect

When someone visits this website, the site may process basic technical and marketing information such as page views, browser and device information, approximate location derived from network data, referring pages, campaign parameters, and click activity on Early Access calls to action.

If a visitor continues to the Seinote signup page, attribution parameters such as UTM values, gclid, gbraid and wbraid may be preserved in the redirect URL so advertising performance can be measured.

Cookies And Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies, pixels, browser storage, tags and similar technologies for measurement, attribution, diagnostics and advertising analytics. These technologies help understand whether marketing campaigns are working and whether visitors interact with Early Access calls to action.

Google Ads, Meta Pixel And Advertising Analytics

This website includes Google Ads tracking for landing page and Apply click measurement. It may also include Meta Pixel PageView tracking when configured.

CompleteRegistration is not fired from this landing page. That event is intended to be fired only after a real account creation is confirmed in the signup application.

Meta Marketing API

Seinote may use the Meta Marketing API to create, manage and measure advertising campaigns for the public Early Access website. This use is limited to advertising operations, campaign setup, creative delivery, measurement and related reporting.

Why Information Is Processed

Information is processed to operate the public website, understand campaign performance, preserve advertising attribution, improve the Early Access experience, protect against duplicate tracking, troubleshoot technical issues and support responsible marketing decisions.

Third-Party Providers

This website may rely on third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising measurement, advertising delivery and signup redirection. Providers may include Vercel, Google Ads, Meta, Supabase and the Seinote application domain.

Sharing Of Information

Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the website, measure advertising, preserve attribution, troubleshoot performance or support campaign reporting. This policy does not state that Seinote sells personal information.

Retention

Marketing and diagnostic information should be kept only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required for security, operational, legal or accounting reasons.

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect the website and related marketing systems. No public website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Health Information And Clinical Records

This public marketing website is not intended to collect medical records, clinical notes, protected health information, diagnosis details, therapy session content or client files. Visitors should not submit patient or clinical information through this website.

User Rights

Depending on location, visitors may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or information about how their personal information is handled. Requests can be sent to hello@seinote.ca.

Contact

Privacy questions, access requests, correction requests or deletion requests can be sent to hello@seinote.ca.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated as the website, advertising setup or signup flow changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
